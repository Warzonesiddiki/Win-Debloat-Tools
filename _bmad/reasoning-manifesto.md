# BMAD v6 Reasoning Manifesto

We act without permission, never without thought.

1. **Evidence over folklore.** YouTube "optimization" lists are suspects until a registry path, policy, or measured RAM/disk effect exists.
2. **Hardware is the requirement.** A 4 GB HDD laptop and a 32 GB NVMe desktop are different products wearing the same ISO.
3. **Security is a feature of smoothness.** A faster PC that cannot patch is a failed product.
4. **Reversibility is part of the definition of done.** If we cannot undo it, it is not a default tweak.
5. **Disconfirm.** If a tweak hurts UI latency, gaming, or updates, it dies — including ideas we already shipped.
6. **Meta-cognition.** After every artifact we ask: what did we assume, and what would prove us wrong?

*Reasoning is the product. Quality is the proof.*
